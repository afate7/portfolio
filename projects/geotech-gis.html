Overview

GeoTech builds geospatial solutions for the public sector. I own the end-to-end product roadmap, setting vision and strategy across product, design, and research, and owning delivery with engineering by turning technical constraints into scope decisions the business can act on.

The flagship is a geospatial decision-support system (DSS) that gives non-technical government staff direct access to spatial analysis for planning and decisions that previously required dedicated GIS specialists.

The challenge

GIS is hard in a way most software isn't, the complexity is in the domain itself, not in legacy or regulation. Spatial data demands a different approach to storage, querying, and processing, and the interface has to display layers of reality without overwhelming the people using it. The goal was to hide that complexity behind an experience a planner could actually use.

The approach

Working with engineering, we drove a privacy-first data design: splitting a data lake for analytics from a PostGIS store for live map operations, and using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to feed the language model only database schema from a vector database, so it could generate spatial queries without ever touching client data.

I lead a team of 20 across product, design, development, QC, and research, translating government needs into a roadmap the whole group builds against, and leaning on AI-powered tooling to sharpen product intelligence and decision-making.

Impact

  • A geospatial DSS live inside several government entities
  • Multi-million-dollar deals with municipalities and government institutions
  • Spatial analysis put directly in the hands of non-specialists
