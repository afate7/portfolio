Tameeni, Rebuilding Insurance from the Inside

Category
InsurTech
Year
2024

Senior Product Manager → Research Manager
Team
Design & research team
  • ⭐ 4.7★ from 157,000+ ratings, 1M+ downloads
  • 📈 +10% revenue from new insurance products
  • 📐 Design thinking embedded org-wide

The Situation

Insurance products are hard to love. The category is built around legal language, anxious moments, and transactions people make hoping they'll never need the outcome. Tameeni, Saudi Arabia's leading motor-insurance platform, had a technically sound product and a strong market position. What it didn't have was a systematic way of hearing its users.

Starting on the Business Side

I joined Rasan, Tameeni's parent company, as a Senior Product Manager focused on the Saudi market: regulatory compliance audits, market research, and portfolio strategy. That work produced tangible results, we pioneered new insurance products that diversified the portfolio and contributed to a 10% revenue increase, but it also surfaced a bigger gap. Product decisions across the organization leaned on opinion and precedent. User evidence rarely made it into the room.

Building the Research Practice

That gap became my job. Promoted to Research Manager, I set out to make research the default input to product decisions rather than an occasional luxury.

We drove design-thinking adoption through hands-on workshops and training, not a one-off offsite, but a working practice product and design teams actually used. I authored and ran research plans combining qualitative interviews with quantitative studies, and paired them with behavioral analysis in GA4 and Hotjar to identify user segments and the pain points each one hit. For the first time, the organization could see where users struggled and which users they were.

The Revamp

With that foundation in place, I led the design and research team through a revamp of the flagship product. Every significant screen decision traced back to a research finding: where people hesitated, what language confused them, which steps they abandoned. Insurance is a trust purchase, and the redesign treated every moment of friction as a trust question, not just a usability one.

Results

  • The revamped app is the version live today: rated 4.7★ by 157,000+ users, with 1M+ downloads
  • +10% revenue from new insurance products that diversified the portfolio
  • Design thinking embedded across product and design through workshops and training
  • Research plans and GA4/Hotjar behavioral analysis became standard practice, not a special request

The biggest outcome wasn't a screen. It was an organization that stopped guessing.
