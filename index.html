My story

Engineering roots.
Product soul.

It started with code — and a lot of it. I spent the early years of my career as a full-stack developer, writing systems and learning that software's hardest problems are rarely technical. They're human. That realization changed everything.

So I moved upstream — into business analysis, then product strategy, then leading entire product organizations. Thirteen years and four verticals later — GovTech, FinTech, EdTech, and Insurance — the thread running through every project has been the same: people deserve software that respects their time and earns their trust.

Today I work at the intersection of strategy and execution, helping organizations stop building the wrong things — and build the right ones faster, with more confidence.
