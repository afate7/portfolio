My story

Engineering roots.
Product soul.

It started with code, and a lot of it. I spent the early years of my career as a full-stack developer, writing systems and learning that software's hardest problems are rarely technical. They're human. That realization changed everything.

So I moved upstream, into business analysis, then product strategy, then leading entire product organizations. Thirteen years and seven verticals later, FinTech, EdTech, GovTech, Insurance, wellness, e-commerce, and now GIS, the thread running through every project has been the same: people deserve software that respects their time and earns their trust.

Today I work at the intersection of strategy and execution, helping organizations stop building the wrong things, and build the right ones faster, with more confidence. On the side, I build and run SuperCV, an AI SaaS I designed, engineered, and shipped solo. And there are things my co-workers don't usually know about me: I started my own e-commerce company and got 70 clients, then it collapsed. I'm a trader and a junior technical analyst with expertise in the crypto market since 2020. And I read about psychology maybe more than a diploma student does.
